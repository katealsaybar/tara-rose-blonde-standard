It is not you

Five quiet things are changing your hair every day in this climate.

We cannot change the climate. We can change the routine.

Sun

UV breaks down colour molecules, cooler pigments first, so colour shifts warm and loses dimension faster. Not dramatic sun exposure: the cumulative effect of walking between a building and a car every day.

Pool and sea

Chlorine accelerates colour breakdown and dries the cuticle, and salt dries the strand. Not reasons to stop swimming. Reasons to add a protect layer before and after.

Hard water

Each wash deposits a thin mineral layer that builds up and oxidises tone from the outside in. The toner has not failed. The minerals have done what minerals do.

Air conditioning

Year-round AC removes humidity from the air, and hair loses moisture continuously. Over weeks the cuticle roughens and the blonde dulls.

SPF staining

Some sun protection interacts with the colour itself and shows as warmth at the hairline.

Most blonde advice is written for soft-water, moderate-UV climates. This is not one of them, and nobody handed you the version written for here. You are not doing anything wrong.

The environment moves your colour. Your plan holds it.

Questions

The honest answers.

How do I know which lane I am?
Soft Blonde is lower-maintenance brightness with softer grow-out. Signature Blonde is a regular blonde rhythm for women who identify as blonde. Transformation Blonde is the high-protection lane for major change, high lift, or correction. And you do not have to be right: the Confidence Mapping names it with you.
How often will I need to come in?
The protect visit sits at week six to eight, with seven as the default: earlier for high-impact blonde, later for lived-in and stable. The top-up sits at week twelve. Your stylist names your exact week in the chair, and you leave with the next two visits booked.
Will lightening damage my hair?
Healthy blonde is possible when protection is built in from the start and maintained properly afterwards. Lightening leaves the strand more sensitive, which is why bond protection works through every blonde service here, before, during and after your colour, and why condition is treated as part of the blonde rather than an afterthought.
Why does my blonde keep going warm?
When your blonde goes brassy, it usually has not gone wrong at all. The cool finish has simply worn off, and the warmer canvas underneath, the natural pigment that was always there, is showing through again. It looks like the colour failed. It is actually the most predictable thing hair does.
