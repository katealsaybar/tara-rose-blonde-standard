It is not you

Five quiet things change your hair every day here.

Most blonde advice is written for soft-water, moderate-UV climates. This is not one of them.

Sun

UV works on lightened hair every day, not only at the beach.

Pool

Chlorine sits in porous blonde and dulls the finish.

Hard water

Mineral-heavy water works on the toner from the outside in.

Air conditioning

Dry air pulls moisture, and lightened hair gives it up first.

SPF

Some sun protection transfers onto blonde and leaves warmth at the hairline.

A toner wears the way a topcoat wears on a manicure. Nothing failed. A finish reached the end of its life, and in this climate it gets there sooner. So when your colour shifts, your hair did not let you down and neither did you. Something moved it, and a plan accounts for that.

The environment moves your colour. Your plan holds it.

The rhythm

Blonde is not one appointment. It never was.

You were not forgetting to book. Nobody ever gave you the plan. This is the plan: three touchpoints, on a loop.

Touchpoint oneCreate

The blonde itself. The lift, the placement, the tone, with bond protection at every stage. You leave with the next two visits already booked.

Touchpoint twoProtect and maintain

Hydration and blonde longevity, and your toner refreshed before it shifts warm, not after.

Touchpoint threeTop-up

Tone and shine refreshed, protection topped up, and the next cycle booked before you leave.

How far apart? That is yours, not a template. Your stylist gives you your weeks in the chair, and they are written into your plan.

Questions

The honest answers.

How do I know which lane I am?
You will probably recognise it in the three descriptions further up, and you do not have to be right. The consultation names it with you: your stylist looks at what your hair has been through and where you want it to live, and the lane follows from that.
How often will I need to come in?
That is exactly what your plan answers, and it is yours, not a template. Your stylist gives you your weeks in the chair, written down, based on your lane, your hair and how bright you keep it.
Will lightening damage my hair?
Lightening leaves the strand more sensitive, which is why bond protection, Fibreplex, Olaplex or K18, works through every stage of every lightening service here, and why condition is treated as part of the blonde rather than an afterthought.
Why does my blonde keep going warm?
Because the cool finish wears, and the warm canvas underneath shows through. The environment here moves it faster: sun, pool, hard water, air conditioning and SPF all push at it every day. A toner refresh before it shifts, rather than after, is what holds it.
Which salon should I choose?
The one you can get to regularly, because the plan only works if the visits actually happen. Tell us your closest salon on the form and we confirm your stylist and your booking there before anything is fixed.
What does it cost?
It depends on your lane, your hair’s length and history, and what your first appointment involves, which is why the consultation comes first and is free. You leave knowing your plan and what each part of it costs, before you commit to anything.
What if it does not feel right afterwards?
You are covered by the 7-Day Confidence Promise, in full, further up this page.
